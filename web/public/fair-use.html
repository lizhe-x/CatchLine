Fair Use Policy

Version 1 · Effective October 11, 2026

Live captioning has a real per-minute cost for us. These limits keep CatchLine affordable while staying out of the way of normal use.

Current allowances

  • Free: 3 minutes per day, resetting at midnight in your time zone.
  • Free trial (7 days, monthly or yearly plan; one trial per Apple Account): up to 3 hours in total. If you use it up early, you can start your paid year right away.
  • Pro (monthly or yearly): 40 hours per calendar month (UTC), with no daily limit. That's about two hours every working day; almost no one reaches it.

You can always see what's left in the app.

Changes to these limits

  • Changes in your favor (more time, lower prices) may take effect immediately.
  • Reductions are announced in the app at least 30 days in advance and apply from your next renewal. You can cancel before then and won't be charged again.
  • If we make a major change, we may introduce a new plan and let existing subscribers keep their current terms.

Misuse

Allowances are for personal use. Automated use, reselling access, or tampering with the app may result in access being limited.

合理使用政策

第 1 版 · 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 11 日

实时字幕每一分钟都有真实成本。以下额度既让 CatchLine 保持实惠，也基本不会影响正常使用。

当前额度

  • 免费版：每天 3 分钟，在你所在时区的午夜重置。
  • 免费试用（月付或年付，7 天；每个 Apple 账户限一次）：总计最多 3 小时。提前用完可以立即开始付费年度。
  • Pro（月付或年付）：每个自然月（UTC）40 小时，不限每日用量，约等于每个工作日 2 小时，几乎不会用到上限。

你可以随时在 App 里查看剩余额度。

额度调整

  • 对你有利的调整（增加时长、降价）可能立即生效。
  • 减少额度会至少提前 30 天在 App 内通知，并从你的下一个续费周期开始生效。你可以在此之前取消，不会再被扣费。
  • 如有重大调整，我们可能推出新方案，并让已有订阅用户保留原有条件。

滥用
