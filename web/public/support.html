Catch every word.

CatchLine shows live captions and translations of the speech around you: put a call on speaker, join a meeting, or talk face to face, and read along in your own language.

Getting started

  1. Open CatchLine and tap Start. Allow microphone access.
  2. Put the conversation on speaker (or hold the phone near the speaker). Captions appear within a second or two; the translation follows right below.
  3. In Settings, choose the language you're listening to and the language to translate into. Add names and terms under Keywords for better accuracy.
  4. Tap Stop when you're done, then Export to save the transcript.

Plans

  • Free: 3 minutes a day.
  • Pro: monthly or yearly, 40 hours a month (fair use). New subscribers get a 7-day free trial with 3 hours included.

Manage or cancel your subscription any time in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. If you reinstall or switch phones, tap Restore Purchases in CatchLine's settings.

Can CatchLine caption calls on the same iPhone?

iOS doesn't let apps hear phone-call audio. Put the call on speaker on another phone and let CatchLine listen, or use CatchLine on a second device.

Privacy Policy · Fair Use Policy

帮助与支持

CatchLine 为你身边的语音提供实时字幕和翻译：电话开外放、开会、面对面交谈，都能用自己的语言跟上。

快速上手

  1. 打开 CatchLine，点开始，允许使用麦克风。
  2. 把对话开外放（或把手机靠近扬声器），一两秒内出现字幕，翻译紧跟在下面。
  3. 在设置里选择听的语言和翻译成的语言；在关键词里填人名和术语可以提高准确率。
  4. 结束时点停止，再点导出保存文字。

方案

  • 免费版：每天 3 分钟。
  • Pro：月付或年付，每月 40 小时（合理使用）。新订阅用户享 7 天免费试用，含 3 小时。

可随时在 设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅 中管理或取消。重装或换手机后，在 CatchLine 设置里点恢复购买。

能给同一台 iPhone 上的电话加字幕吗？

iOS 不允许 App 获取通话音频。请在另一台手机上开外放让 CatchLine 收听，或在第二台设备上使用 CatchLine。
